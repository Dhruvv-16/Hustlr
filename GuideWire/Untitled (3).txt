## 🎯 Why Q-Commerce? Why Zepto?

We chose Zepto/Q-Commerce over food delivery (Zomato/Swiggy) for four evidence-backed reasons:

**1. Higher income volatility per disruption event**
Q-commerce workers operate in tight 10-minute delivery radius zones. A 2-hour rain event wipes a proportionally larger share of their daily earnings because peak earning windows are shorter and more concentrated.

**2. Hyperlocal zones already match our parametric model**
Each Zepto dark store's delivery radius (2–3 km) is a natural parametric insurance zone. Our trigger model maps directly onto this architecture — no artificial grid needed.

**3. Dark-store-specific disruptions are completely unaddressed**
When a dark store closes due to flooding or power failure, every worker assigned to it loses 100% income for that period. No existing product covers this. We do.

**4. Fastest-growing segment, most underserved**
Q-commerce is India's fastest-growing delivery segment (projected 3× growth by 2026) with zero income protection products. First-mover advantage is highest here.

## 💡 What ShieldGig Does

ShieldGig is an **AI-powered parametric income insurance platform** that:

1. 🔍 **Monitors** disruptions in Karthik's dark store zone every 30 minutes via verified external APIs
2. 🔮 **Predicts** disruptions 48–72 hours ahead and nudges Karthik to activate coverage before they hit
3. ⚡ **Triggers** a claim automatically the moment a threshold is breached — Karthik does nothing
4. 🛡️ **Validates** the claim through an 8-signal AI fraud engine before any payout is released
5. 💰 **Pays** Karthik's wallet every Sunday night — matching his weekly income cycle exactly

> **The Zero Submission Principle:** Workers never submit claims, upload evidence, or request payouts. Every trigger, validation, and payout is system-initiated. There is no submission interface to game.

## 💰 Weekly Premium Model — Detailed

### Why Weekly?

Zepto partners receive platform earnings weekly. Our premium cycle aligns structurally with their income cycle — workers pay Monday morning and receive any triggered payout Sunday night. Insurance feels like a natural part of their earnings rhythm, not an added burden.

### Four Plan Tiers

| Plan | Weekly Premium | Max Weekly Payout | Triggers/Week | Coverage |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Basic Shield** | ₹35 – ₹55 | ₹400 | 1 | Rain, Extreme Heat |
| **Standard Shield** | ₹55 – ₹85 | ₹700 | 2 | Rain, Heat, Pollution, App Downtime |
| **Full Shield** | ₹80 – ₹130 | ₹1,200 | Unlimited | All disruption types |
| **Elite Shield** | ₹140 – ₹200 | ₹2,000 | Unlimited + Compound | All types + compound + 10% cashback* |

*Elite Shield: 4 consecutive claim-free weeks → 10% of premiums returned as wallet credit.*

### Premium Formula

weekly_premium = base_premium × risk_multiplier × tier_factor

risk_multiplier  = 0.8 + (AI_risk_score × 1.7)     [range: 0.8 – 2.5]

base_premiums    = { basic: 35, standard: 55, full: 80, elite: 140 }
tier_factors     = { basic: 1.0, standard: 1.2, full: 1.5, elite: 1.8 }

Affordability cap:  premium ≤ 3% of weekly_income_estimate


### Add-On Riders

| Rider | Weekly Cost | Covers |
|---|---|---|
| Curfew & Strike | +₹15 | Local shutdowns, bandhs |
| Election Day | +₹20 | Reduced orders on polling days |
| App Downtime | +₹12 | Platform outages over 90 minutes |
| Cyclone Cover | +₹25 | Enhanced coastal storm coverage |

### Weekly Settlement Cycle

Monday 06:00 AM  ── Premium auto-deducted from wallet
                    Push: "₹72 deducted. You're covered this week."

Mon – Sun        ── Trigger engine polls APIs every 30 min
                    Disruption detected → Claim auto-created (PENDING)
                    Worker notified: "Rain detected — claim processing"
                    48-72hr nudge: "Heavy rain Friday — activate now"

Sunday 11:00 PM  ── Weekly Settlement cron runs:
                    1. Aggregate all PENDING claims
                    2. Select highest qualifying event per plan rules
                    3. Run 8-signal fraud check on all claims
                    4. Calculate final payout
                    5. Credit 70% → Monday midnight
                    6. Hold 30% → 48hr fraud review window

Tuesday midnight ── Release remaining 30% for clean claims

## 🔐 Adversarial Defense & Anti-Spoofing Strategy

> *Directly addressing the DEVTrails 2026 Market Crash event (March 19, 2026): a coordinated GPS-spoofing syndicate of 500 workers exploited a rival parametric platform via Telegram coordination, draining its liquidity pool with mass false payouts. ShieldGig's architecture was designed with this exact threat model in mind from day one.*

---

### Part 1 — The Differentiation: Genuine Worker vs. GPS Spoofer

Simple GPS verification asks one question: "Is this coordinate inside Zone A?" A spoofing app answers yes regardless of physical location. ShieldGig asks **six simultaneous questions** that spoofed coordinates cannot answer:

**Signal 1: isMockLocation Flag (Android API)**
Flutter's geolocator package reads Android's native isMockLocation boolean on every GPS ping. If any ping during the shift returns true, the entire shift is auto-invalidated. A genuinely stranded worker's phone does not run mock location software.

**Signal 2: Accelerometer Cross-Referencing**
A delivery worker on a motorcycle shows vibration patterns, speed changes, and turning motion. A worker sitting at home shows a flat, stationary accelerometer. GPS reporting movement at 20 kmph while accelerometer shows complete stillness is a physical impossibility — flagged automatically.

**Signal 3: Speed Plausibility Validation**
GPS pings every 5 minutes. Legitimate delivery travel covers 0.4–5 km between pings. If the coordinate delta implies more than 100 kmph, that is a teleportation artifact — a spoofing signature.

**Signal 4: Zone Depth Scoring**

depth_score = mean(distance_from_boundary / zone_radius)
              across all GPS pings during active shift

Payout multiplier by depth_score:
  0.00 – 0.20  →  0.0×   (boundary loitering — no payout)
  0.21 – 0.40  →  0.3×
  0.41 – 0.60  →  0.6×
  0.61 – 0.80  →  0.85×
  0.81 – 1.00  →  1.0×   (genuinely deep in zone — full payout)

Hard rule: worker must have at least one GPS ping in the core zone
           (2km+ from any boundary) within 4 hours before trigger fires


A spoofer standing just inside the zone boundary scores under 0.20 and receives zero payout. A genuine worker delivering across the zone all day scores 0.75–0.90 and receives full payout.

**Signal 5: Rapid Zone Change Detection**
If the worker's location changed zones in the 30 minutes just before the trigger fired, this suggests deliberate repositioning into a high-risk zone. +30 fraud points.

**Signal 6: Platform Activity Corroboration**
Zero order activity logged by the platform during the disruption window despite GPS showing worker in zone means they were not genuinely working. +35 fraud points.

### Part 2 — The Data: Detecting a Coordinated Fraud Ring

| Data Point | Signal | Threshold |
|---|---|---|
| **Registration Cluster** | 20+ accounts created in same zone within 48 hrs | Elevated monitoring on entire cohort |
| **GPS Density Anomaly** | 8+ workers GPS-pinging within 100m² simultaneously | Fraud ring alert + cohort block |
| **Claim Synchronization** | Worker A and B claimed same 3+ events | Pairwise coordination flag |
| **Device Fingerprint** | Same hardware ID across multiple accounts | Farm detection — instant flag |
| **UPI VPA Clustering** | Multiple accounts share same UPI mobile number | Proxy account detection |
| **Weather Cross-Validation** | Claim vs IMD + OpenWeather + satellite historical | Three-source mismatch = hard block |

**Regional Behavioral Intelligence (RBI) Layer:**
Chennai gig workers are exceptionally financially literate and organize in WhatsApp/Telegram groups to share platform strategies. ShieldGig's RBI Layer scrapes public Reddit (r/Chennai, r/zomato) and Twitter/X weekly using the PRAW API. NLP identifies emerging exploitation patterns. When a new gaming behavior is detected, fraud signal weights update within 24 hours — staying ahead of exploits rather than reacting after losses.

## 📱 Platform Choice — Mobile Flutter

We chose Flutter over a web app for one non-negotiable reason: **background GPS on budget Android phones.**

| Capability | Flutter | Web App |
|---|---|---|
| Background GPS screen off | ✅ Yes | ❌ Never |
| Works on budget Android | ✅ Consistent | ⚠️ Unreliable |
| isMockLocation detection | ✅ Yes | ❌ No |
| Accelerometer access | ✅ Yes | ⚠️ Limited |
| Offline + auto-sync | ✅ Built-in | ⚠️ Limited |
| Push notifications | ✅ FCM native | ⚠️ Browser permission |

Our entire fraud detection system depends on continuous GPS tracking while the worker is on shift — including when the screen is off. A web app cannot do this. Zepto partners use ₹8,000–₹15,000 Android phones (Redmi, Realme, Narzo) — Flutter performs measurably better on these devices than React Native for background-intensive tasks.

what is exactly pwa

A PWA (Progressive Web App) is essentially:

a website that behaves like an app

It runs in the browser but tries to mimic features of a mobile app.

🧠 Core Idea
Instead of installing from Play Store/App Store, a PWA:

opens via URL (like a website)

can be “installed” to home screen

runs in a standalone window (no browser UI)

⚙️ What makes a web app a PWA
A normal website becomes a PWA when it has 3 things:

1. Web App Manifest
A JSON file that defines:

app name

icon

theme

launch behavior

This is what allows:

“Add to Home Screen”

2. Service Worker
A background script that enables:

caching (offline support)

faster loading

background sync (limited)

3. HTTPS
Required for security (mandatory)

📱 What it can do
PWA capabilities:

Install on phone (like app icon)

Work offline (cached pages)

Send basic push notifications

Run full screen (no browser UI)

❌ What it cannot do well (important)
This is where confusion happens.

PWA is NOT equal to native app.

Limitations:

❌ Background GPS tracking (very unreliable)

❌ Access to device integrity (no Play Integrity)

❌ Limited sensor access (accelerometer, gyroscope)

❌ Weak background execution (gets killed)

❌ Restricted system-level APIs

⚖️ PWA vs Native App (quick clarity)
Feature	PWA	Native (Flutter/Android)
Install	via browser	Play Store
Performance	good	best
Background tasks	❌ limited	✅ full
GPS tracking	❌ weak	✅ strong
Device security APIs	❌ none	✅ available
Sensors	⚠️ limited	✅ full access
🧩 In your case (very important)
Your project needs:

continuous tracking

fraud detection signals

device-level validation

These are:

👉 native-level requirements

So:

PWA = okay for demo
Native = required for real product

🧠 Simple analogy
Website → like opening Chrome tab

PWA → like “website pretending to be an app”

Native app → real app with full device access

🏁 Final understanding
A PWA is a web app enhanced with installability, offline support, and limited app-like features — but it cannot fully replace native apps for system-level functionality.
